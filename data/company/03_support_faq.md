A: You can request a refund within **30 days** of purchase. *(STALE — update to 14 days.)*

**Q: Are annual plans refundable?**
A: Yes, pro rata for unused full months if requested within the eligibility window.

**Q: Who approves large refunds?**
A: Refunds over $5,000 need Finance approval (see Finance Refund Processing SOP).

**Q: Do enterprise customers have different terms?**
A: Some enterprise agreements have explicit terms. Check the contract before promising a window — e.g., ACME Corp has a signed 30-day guarantee through Dec 31, 2026.
