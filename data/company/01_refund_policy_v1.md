## Policy statement

Northstar Cloud offers a **30-day refund eligibility window** from the date of purchase for all self-serve plans. Annual plans are refunded pro rata for unused full months. Enterprise agreements with explicit refund terms take precedence over this general policy.

## Scope

- Applies to self-serve and standard contracts signed before October 15, 2026, unless the contract references the general policy dynamically.
- Enterprise agreements with explicit refund clauses (e.g., ACME Corp Enterprise Agreement) override this policy.

## Conditions

1. Refund requests must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the original charge.
2. Usage-based overages are non-refundable after consumption.
3. Refunds over $5,000 require Finance approval.
