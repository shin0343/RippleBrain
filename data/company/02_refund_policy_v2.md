# Refund Policy v2 — 14 Days (CURRENT)

**Status:** CURRENT general policy, effective **October 15, 2026**. Supersedes Refund Policy v1.

## Policy statement

Northstar Cloud offers a **14-day refund eligibility window** from the date of purchase for all self-serve plans. Annual plans are refunded pro rata for unused full months.

## What changed vs v1

- Eligibility window: **30 days → 14 days**.
- Effective date: **October 15, 2026**.
- Finance approval threshold unchanged ($5,000).
- Enterprise agreements with explicit refund terms continue to override this general policy through their stated expiry.

## Scope and exceptions

- Applies to new purchases and renewals on or after October 15, 2026.
- Does NOT automatically override signed enterprise agreements with explicit refund terms (see ACME Corp Enterprise Agreement, guaranteed 30-day refunds through December 31, 2026).
- Standard Customer Agreement follows the general policy dynamically and therefore adopts the 14-day window.
